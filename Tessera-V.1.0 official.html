Import into Ableton Live

  1. Download the file (saves to your Downloads folder).
  2. Drag it onto an audio track in the Arrangement view, or drop it into a clip slot in Session view.
  3. Double‑click the clip to open Clip View, then chop, slice, and warp from there.

Tip: In Clip View, set Warp to “Beats” for drums, or “Complex” for melodic loops. Changes tempo without pitch‑shifting.
